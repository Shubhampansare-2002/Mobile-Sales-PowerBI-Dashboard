# 📱 Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Mobile Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze mobile phone sales performance across different cities, brands, mobile models, payment methods, customer ratings, and time periods.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of key sales KPIs and enables users to explore sales trends and customer behavior through interactive filters and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall mobile sales performance.
- Track total sales, quantity sold, transactions, and average sales.
- Identify top-performing mobile brands and models.
- Analyze sales performance across different cities.
- Understand customer ratings and their distribution.
- Analyze transactions by payment method.
- Identify monthly and daily sales trends.
- Provide interactive filtering for better business analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Total Sales by City
A geographical visualization showing mobile sales distribution across different cities.

### 2. Total Quantity by Month
A line chart showing monthly quantity sold and helping identify seasonal sales trends.

### 3. Customer Ratings
A funnel-style visualization displaying the distribution of customer ratings from 1 to 5.

### 4. Transactions by Payment Method
A pie/donut chart showing the percentage of transactions made through different payment methods.

### 5. Mobile Sales Details
A detailed table containing:

- Brand
- Transactions
- Mobile Model
- Total Quantity
- Total Sales

### 6. Day-wise Total Sales
A trend visualization showing total sales across different days of the week.

### 7. Total Sales by Mobile Model
A bar chart comparing sales performance of different mobile models.
